Everything you reach for, built in.

SparkyTube wraps the YouTube mobile site in a faster, cleaner player and adds the things the official app keeps behind a subscription or leaves out.

Watching

Ads blocked
Video and page ads are filtered out before they load.
Sponsor segments skipped
Uses the SponsorBlock community database to skip sponsors, self-promos and like-and-subscribe reminders.
Chapters
Chapter marks on the seek bar when the description has timestamps. Tap the chapter name to jump.
Resume where you stopped
Open a video again and it carries on from your last position.

Controls

Swipe for volume and brightness
Slide up or down on the video, with an on-screen indicator.
Double-tap to seek
Skip forward or back without reaching for the seek bar.
Music mode
Turn the picture off and keep listening, even with the screen locked.
Speed and quality
Change playback speed and video quality from the player.

Downloading

Video or audio only
Download from the player, or long-press a video in the feed.
Pick the quality
Higher qualities are downloaded as separate video and audio and joined on your phone.
Offline library
Watch what you saved inside the app. Files also land in your Downloads folder.
Progress at a glance
One screen shows every download with progress and speed.

Streams are resolved on your phone.

No middle server to go down. SparkyTube runs yt-dlp inside the app and keeps it loaded, so the second video you open is much quicker than the first.

  • yt-dlp Data SaverOn by default: plays at 360p to save mobile data. Switch it off in Settings to get every quality yt-dlp finds.
  • Crash screen with logsIf something breaks, you get a log you can share instead of a silent close.
  • Material YouOptional dynamic colors from your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer.

Install it in a minute.

SparkyTube isn't on the Play Store, so you install the APK file yourself.

  1. Download

    Pick the file for your phone below. If you aren't sure, take arm64-v8a.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks once whether your browser or file manager may install apps. Say yes.

  3. Open and tap Install

    Android may warn about an unknown source, that's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

Questions

Is this the official YouTube app?

No. SparkyTube is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Do I need to sign in?

No. You can watch without an account. Sign in inside the app if you want your own subscriptions and recommendations.

Does it cost anything?

No, it's free.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. SparkyTube is built for Android only.

Where do my downloads go?

Into your phone's Downloads folder, and they also show up in the app's Offline Library.

A video won't play. What now?

Open Settings, then Logs, and share the log with the developer on Telegram. It shows exactly which step failed.
